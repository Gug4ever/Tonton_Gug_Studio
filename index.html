About Limouze Books

Limouze Books is an independent imprint dedicated to stories and ideas that linger. We publish children's biographies, literary non-fiction, and author-led projects that cross borders — in language, genre, and imagination. Every book we publish is a deliberate act of curiosity.

Daisy Harper
Gray-Lane

Daisy Harper Gray-Lane is the author of the Brilliant Women, Remarkable Stories series — eight illustrated biographies celebrating remarkable women across history and culture. Her books are designed to inspire young readers aged 5–9, bringing history to life through warm, accessible storytelling and vibrant illustration.

Literary Fiction & Non-Fiction

William
Loxford

William Loxford is a New Zealand-born, internationally raised writer whose work moves freely between reflection, storytelling, and essay — shaped by a life lived across the United Kingdom, Spain, France, and Hong Kong. His writing blends humour, observation, and literary curiosity without ever settling into a single category.
